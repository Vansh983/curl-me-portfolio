# 70. Forward over Halifax

Moving the passenger to the left window had also turned the moving ground through 180 degrees. A fixed building slid toward the aircraft’s nose as the flight advanced.

The ground now moves toward the tail along the cabin’s own axis. The mapped approach passes west of the Macdonald Bridge and finishes with Goldberg 120 metres off the left side. From the seat, the bridge passes behind the wing and the closer rooftops pass the window on the descent. The flight covers the same 1,850 metres at the same pace.

Headless Chrome tracked the bridge relative to the cabin at successive scroll positions. Walk mode carried the passenger from the jet bridge into the auditorium. Frames and the Walk recording are in `.cache/round70/`.
