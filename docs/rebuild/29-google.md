# Google, 2019: the boardroom and the balcony (2026-09-13)

He asked for the Google chapter redesigned: "I walk outside, I see Google San Francisco, I go toward Google's office, inside I get the award." Then: the award was at the Google Sunnyvale office, in a boardroom; the frame must show San Francisco (the Golden Gate), Google, and "Grand Prize Winner"; a balcony overlooking the bridge is fine. Sunnyvale cannot see the Golden Gate: this is a postcard composite by his choice. The Google part is one chapter, no more.

## The story's beat
Delhi bedroom, Delhi lab, then the first door out of India: light, the award, and straight back into the dark Covid room. The chapter's job is that contrast.

## The set (set 3, `google`, replacing the Embarcadero plaza)
- Out of the lab's south passage straight into a boardroom, 7.2 by 7.8 m (x 0.8..8.0, z -5.2..2.6), carpet, white walls, a tile ceiling. A long table with eight chairs; the trophy on its near corner; a wall screen at the far end reading "Google Code-in 2018 · Grand Prize Winner · Vansh Sood". Nothing else written.
- The south wall is glass, floor to ceiling, one slider open. A balcony beyond it, 3 m deep, a glass balustrade, 12 m over the bay.
- The bay: water to the horizon, the Golden Gate 1.06 km off at 1:2 (real dimensions halved: towers 227 m, span 1,280 m, side spans 343 m, deck 67 m up, international orange), seen from Crissy Field's angle, the Marin Headlands and the Presidio bluffs behind and beside it from real elevation (Mapzen Terrarium), a June morning sun behind the viewer.
- The walk: down the passage, into the room, to the table's corner where the trophy rises into the hands (as the degree does), a look across the table at the screen, then south through the open slider onto the balcony: the bridge, the trophy in hand. That frame holds, and the story cuts (a portal, like the phone's) to inside the 2020 Delhi room. No walk back.
- Photos of the trip go on the north wall in frames when he sends them; until then the wall is bare.
