# Tour facts and assets

Researched 2026-09-26. Web only. Every URL below was opened (page, API record, or image).

## 1. Verdict

- Vancouver hero: Canada Place sails against the North Shore mountains. Best asset is weak (stylised, 2007). The five sails are simple to build by hand.
- Toronto hero: CN Tower. Best asset: Gula's CN Tower, CC BY 4.0, textured, 3.5 MB.
- Montreal hero: the coloured glass facade of the Palais des congres (it is the ALL IN venue). Asset on 3D Warehouse.
- Halifax hero: Town Clock on Citadel Hill, about 0.2 km from Volta. Asset on 3D Warehouse.
- Licence risk: 3D Warehouse models are NOT Creative Commons. See 7.1 before shipping any of them.
- True CC models of named places: CN Tower, Habitat 67, Montreal metro sign and station, Halifax Province House. Everything else city-specific is 3D Warehouse or open city data.
- Smithsonian 3D and Wikimedia Commons 3D: nothing usable for these cities.

## 2. How things were checked

- Facts: page text opened through a fetch tool. Numbers are as the page gives them.
- Colours of vehicles and the Volta interior: checked by opening the photos themselves.
- Sketchfab: read from the Sketchfab API model records (licence, faces, GLB size, animation count). Thumbnails opened where marked "seen".
- 3D Warehouse: read from the 3D Warehouse API entity records (polygons, GLB size). Thumbnails opened where marked "seen".
- "not seen" means the record was read but the picture was not opened.

## 3. Vancouver

### Event and venue
- Web Summit Vancouver: May 27 to 30, 2025, Vancouver Convention Centre, more than 15,000 people from over 130 countries (https://www.britishcolumbia.ca/news-stories/web-summit-vancouver-2025-highlights-b-c-innovation/)
- Address given: 1055 Canada Pl (https://www.accessnow.org/event/access-now-at-web-summit-vancouver/)
- 1,108 startups from 117 countries (https://vancouver.websummit.com/blog/news/web-summit-vancouver-debuts-with-record-breaking-1108-startups-from-117-countries/)
- Which building (East or West) held which stage: unverified. The venue page shows unfilled template fields (https://www.vancouverconventioncentre.com/events/web-summit-vancouver)
- West building: opened April 4, 2009. LMN Architects with MCM and DA. LEED Platinum. Living roof 2.6 hectares, modelled on coastal grassland, slopes 3 to 56 percent (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver_Convention_Centre)
- Roof holds more than 400,000 indigenous plants and grasses. BC wood used throughout. Seawater heating and cooling. Marine habitat built into the foundation (https://www.vancouverconventioncentre.com/about-us/sustainability)
- About 35 percent of the building stands on piles over water. 400,000 sq ft of walkways, bikeways and plazas. Roof folds to keep view corridors to the water (https://lmnarchitects.com/project/vancouver-convention-centre-west)
- East building sits inside Canada Place, opened 1986 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver_Convention_Centre)

### What a walker sees
- Canada Place: five white sails, 27 fibreglass sheets coated in Teflon, stretched on steel cables, held by 40 foot steel masts (https://www.panpacificvancouver.com/canada-place-replaces-its-iconic-five-sails/)
- Canada Place: opened May 2, 1986, at 999 Canada Place on Burrard Inlet, main cruise terminal. Ten horns play the first four notes of O Canada at noon (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Place)
- North Shore Mountains: the city backdrop, forested slopes. The Lions 1,654 m (twin granite domes), Grouse 1,231 m, Seymour 1,449 m (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Shore_Mountains)
- Jack Poole Plaza, Coal Harbour: Olympic Cauldron with reflecting pool, and Digital Orca (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Poole_Plaza)
- Digital Orca: Douglas Coupland, 2009, black and white cubes, powder-coated aluminium on a stainless steel frame, 8 m (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Orca)
- Olympic Cauldron: 9.4 m tall, four arms of 16 m, steel, polycarbonate and glass (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Winter_Olympics_cauldron)
- The Drop: deep blue raindrop, 20 m, 2009, by Inges Idee, at the foot of Burrard Street (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Drop_(sculpture))
- Seawall: stone wall and path, about 22 km, Coal Harbour around Stanley Park and False Creek. Cyclists and walkers on separate lanes (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seawall_(Vancouver))
- Floatplanes: Vancouver Harbour Flight Centre is just west of Canada Place. Harbour Air flies DHC-2 Beavers. Busiest water aerodrome in Canada (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver_Harbour_Flight_Centre)
- SeaBus: Waterfront Station to Lonsdale Quay, 10 to 12 minutes. Aluminium catamarans, 385 passengers, double ended. White or grey hulls with blue and golden yellow stripes. Burrard Chinook carries Indigenous salmon artwork (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SeaBus)
- Waterfront Station: 1914, red brick with a row of white Ionic columns (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterfront_station_(Vancouver))
- Towers: narrow glass residential towers on wide podiums, set back from the street. View rules since 1989 to 1990 protect sightlines to the mountains (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouverism)
- Gastown Steam Clock: 1977, corner of Cambie and Water Street (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gastown_Steam_Clock)
- Science World: geodesic dome by Bruno Freschi for Expo 86, at the east end of False Creek, not on the venue waterfront (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_World_(Vancouver))

### Light and weather
- Oceanic climate (Cfb). 1,159.5 mm of precipitation a year, 169.5 days with precipitation, 1,937.5 sunshine hours (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_of_Vancouver)
- November to February: more than 70 percent of daytime is fully cloudy (same page)
- May normals 1991 to 2020: high 16.9 C, low 9.0 C, 59.1 mm, 12.7 days with precipitation, 222.5 sunshine hours (same page)
- May 28, 2025: sunrise 05:12, sunset 21:07, day length 15 h 56 min (https://api.sunrise-sunset.org/json?lat=49.2888&lng=-123.1111&date=2025-05-28&formatted=0&tzid=America/Vancouver)
- Colour script basis: grey and wet is the yearly character. The event week is the brighter end: long evenings, about 4 days in 10 with rain.

## 4. Toronto

### Event and venue
- Elevate Festival: October 7 to 9, 2025, Meridian Hall and the St. Lawrence Centre. 10,000+ people, 250+ speakers (https://torontoartsandevents.com/business/elevate-festival-2025-sets-the-stage-for-the-future-of-tech-and-innovation-in-canada/)
- Meridian Hall address: 1 Front St E (https://entrepreneurs.utoronto.ca/event/elevate-festival-2025/)
- Elevate has since renamed itself Nrth. The old site redirects (https://nrth.ca/)
- Meridian Hall: opened October 1, 1960. Architects Earle C. Morgan and Peter Dickinson. Mid-century modern, four storeys, clad in Alabama limestone. Three forms: entrance block, auditorium, fly tower (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meridian_Hall_(Toronto))
- Inside: double-height foyer, coffered ceilings, cantilevered stairs, polished bronze doors, a 15 by 100 foot mural by York Wilson, 3,191 seats (same page)
- The cantilevered entrance canopy: unverified, not on the page.

### What a walker sees
- CN Tower: 553.3 m, opened June 26, 1976. Hollow hexagonal concrete shaft on three legs. 1,330 LED lights since 2007. 290 Bremner Boulevard (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CN_Tower)
- Streetcar: Flexity Outlook, 28 m long, 2.54 m wide, 3.84 m high, five sections, four doors on the right side only, trolley pole and pantograph (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flexity_Outlook_(Toronto))
- Streetcar system: 264 cars, overhead wire at 600 V DC, routes include 501 Queen, 504 King, 510 Spadina (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto_streetcar_system)
- Streetcar colours, from the photo: red body, white lower band and white stripe, black window band. The street in the photo is wet (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flexity_Outlook_(Toronto_streetcar)_(50812138638).jpg)
- Union Station: Beaux-Arts, opened August 6, 1927, 65 Front Street West. 22 columns of Bedford limestone, each 40 feet high. Indiana and Queenston limestone facade (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Station_(Toronto))
- Union Station Great Hall: 250 feet long, 88 feet high, Tennessee marble floor in herringbone, Zumbro stone walls, coffered Guastavino tile ceiling (same page)
- Gooderham (Flatiron) Building: 1892, red brick wedge with a steep copper roof, five floors, 16.7 m, where Front and Wellington meet. Trompe l'oeil mural on the back wall (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gooderham_Building)
- Berczy Park, beside the Gooderham Building: two-tier fountain with 27 cast-iron dogs and one cat, by Claude Cormier, reopened June 28, 2017 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berczy_Park)
- St. Lawrence Market South: built 1845 as City Hall, rebuilt 1850, south of Front Street East (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Lawrence_Market)
- Distillery District: more than forty heritage buildings on ten streets, red brick, brick paving, no cars. Largest group of Victorian industrial buildings in North America (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distillery_District)

### Light and weather
- Humid continental (Dfa). 831 mm of precipitation a year, 2,066.3 sunshine hours (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_and_climate_of_Toronto)
- October normals: high 14.6 C, low 7.7 C, 11.7 rain days, 154.3 sunshine hours (same page)
- October 8, 2025: sunrise 07:22, sunset 18:47, day length 11 h 25 min (https://api.sunrise-sunset.org/json?lat=43.6467&lng=-79.3761&date=2025-10-08&formatted=0&tzid=America/Toronto)
- Colour script basis: low autumn sun, short day, cool air. Limestone and red brick are the ground-level materials.
- Timing of autumn leaf colour in Toronto: unverified.

## 5. Montreal

### Event and venue
- ALL IN, third edition: September 24 and 25, 2025, Palais des congres de Montreal. About 6,000 people expected from over 40 countries (https://congresmtl.com/en/all-in-at-the-palais-des-congres-de-montreal-the-third-edition-of-the-largest-ai-event-in-canada/)
- Palais: first building 1983 by Victor Prus. Expanded 1999 to 2002. North edge of Old Montreal. Place-d'Armes metro station is inside the building (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palais_des_congr%C3%A8s_de_Montr%C3%A9al)
- Facade: 390 glass panels, 332 of them coloured. Daylight throws colour into the lobby. Lit from inside at night (https://glasspaint.com/iconic-glass-structures-palais-de-congres-de-montreal/)
- The coloured wall is the Grand Hall Bleury (https://aedifica.com/en/projects/montreal-convention-centre)
- Lipstick Forest inside: 52 pink concrete trees, Claude Cormier, 2002 (https://www.tclf.org/lipstick-forest)
- La Joute, in the square beside it: bronze fountain by Jean-Paul Riopelle, with a ring of flame on summer evenings (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Joute)

### What a walker sees
- Outside stairs: tens of thousands of spiral staircases on building fronts (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture_of_Montreal)
- Why: late 19th century setback rules. Stairs outside saved floor space and heating (https://www.azuremagazine.com/article/exterior-stairs-montreal-urban-fabric-msdl-laurent-clark/)
- Where: Plateau, Rosemont, Villeray, Hochelaga (https://www.themain.com/history-lesson-montreal-spiral-staircases/)
- Stair material (wrought iron) and the count of 30,000 to 40,000: unverified, search snippet only.
- Greystone: grey limestone facades, built 1730 to 1920 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greystone_(architecture))
- Mount Royal Cross: steel lattice, 31.4 m, 1924, 252 m above the river. LED lit, normally white (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Royal_Cross)
- Farine Five Roses sign: 1948, fifteen letters in two rows, each about 15 feet tall, red fill with white outline, red neon. Cycle of about 22 seconds. The word FLOUR came off in 1977 (https://www.farinefiveroses.ca/a-brief-history-of-the-sign)
- Old Port: over 2 km along the river. Ferris wheel opened 2017, tallest in Canada (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Port_of_Montreal)
- Clock Tower: white, 45 m, 1919 to 1922, four clock faces of 3.7 m (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal_Clock_Tower)
- Old Montreal: cobbled streets, stone masonry, Notre-Dame Basilica (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Montreal)
- Bonsecours Market: two storeys, domed, neoclassical, 1847 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonsecours_Market)
- Habitat 67: Moshe Safdie, 1967, 354 stacked concrete boxes, grey-beige, 12 storeys (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habitat_67)
- Biosphere: Buckminster Fuller dome, 76 m across, 62 m high. The acrylic skin burned on May 20, 1976. Open steel lattice remains (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal_Biosphere)
- Jacques Cartier Bridge: steel truss cantilever, opened May 14, 1930, 3,425.6 m. Lit since 2017 with 2,807 lamps (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Cartier_Bridge)
- Metro: Azur trains, rubber tyres, nine cars, in service since 2016 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MPM-10)
- BIXI: blue bikes, more than 900 stations, many removed for winter (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BIXI_Montr%C3%A9al)

### Light and weather
- Humid continental (Dfb). 1,041 mm of precipitation a year, 217 cm of snow, about 2,050 sunshine hours (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_of_Montreal)
- September normals: high 21.1 C, low 10.9 C, 11.3 days with precipitation, 182.2 sunshine hours (same page)
- September 24, 2025: sunrise 06:43, sunset 18:49, day length 12 h 06 min (https://api.sunrise-sunset.org/json?lat=45.5048&lng=-73.5610&date=2025-09-24&formatted=0&tzid=America/Toronto)
- Leaf colour: southern Quebec turns late September to mid October. Mount Royal peaks early to mid October (https://www.aaa.com/tripcanvas/article/fall-colors-in-montreal-CM559)
- Colour script basis: equinox light, mild air, trees still mostly green with the first colour.

## 6. Halifax

### Event and venue
- Volta: 1800 Argyle Street, Suite 801 (https://voltaeffect.com/about)
- Eighth floor of the Armoyan Centre, beside City Hall. Opened November 14, 2023. About 20,000 sq ft. Views over downtown and the harbour (https://www.thecoast.ca/news-opinion/volta-startup-hub-starts-up-in-a-new-space-beside-city-hall-31909484/)
- 20 company offices, four partner offices, a coworking and event space, meeting rooms, call booths (https://betakit.com/boosted-by-6-8-million-in-government-funding-volta-unveils-its-new-space/)
- The building is the former World Trade Centre (https://entrevestor.com/home/entry/volta-to-move-to-armoyan-centre)
- Conflict: Wikipedia lists the old convention centre at 1650 Argyle Street, built 1983, sold to George Armoyan in 2016 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Trade_and_Convention_Centre)
- Demo Day on 2026-01-15: unverified. Not on Volta's events page (https://voltaeffect.com/events)
- Bean AI named in the 2025 to 2026 Invest Nova Scotia Accelerate cohort, third-party page (https://www.ellty.com/investors/halifax-ai-investors)

- Lights: long white LED strips, plus large ring pendants with a warm glow.
- Columns: square, white. Walls: white.

### What a walker sees
- Town Clock: running since October 20, 1803. Three-tier octagonal tower on a one-storey white clapboard building, Palladian. East slope of Citadel Hill, facing Brunswick Street (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halifax_Town_Clock)
- Citadel Hill: star fort, finished 1856, grassy slopes, noon gun fired daily (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citadel_Hill_(Fort_George))
- Grand Parade: square from 1749 between Barrington and Argyle. City Hall at the north end, St. Paul's at the south, cenotaph in the middle (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Parade_(Halifax))
- City Hall: 1887 to 1890, cream and red sandstone over granite, seven-storey tower. North clock face fixed at 9:04 for the 1917 explosion (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halifax_City_Hall)
- St. Paul's Church: 1750, Georgian, 1749 Argyle Street, oldest building in Halifax (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Paul%27s_Church_(Halifax))
- Argyle Street: rebuilt 2017 as a shared street, pavers, almost no curbs, patios all year (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argyle_Street_(Halifax))
- Boardwalk: about 3 km of heavy timber, from the Seaport to the casino (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halifax_Boardwalk)
- Historic Properties: ten old warehouses of timber, ironstone and granite with sandstone trim (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historic_Properties_(Halifax))
- Bent lampposts: "Fountain" and "Get Drunk, Fall Down", installed 2013 (https://discoverhalifaxns.com/things-to-do/halifax-waterfront/story-behind-halifax-boardwalk-lampposts/)
- Hammocks on the waterfront, and the 21 foot mesh "Sail" at Queen's Marque (https://novascotia.com/blog/your-guide-to-the-halifax-waterfront/)
- The Wave sculpture: unverified, search snippet only.
- Ferry: oldest saltwater ferry service in North America, since 1752. Routes to Alderney Landing and Woodside, about 2 km (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halifax%E2%80%93Dartmouth_Ferry_Service)
- Ferry vessel: 24 m long, 9.45 m beam, 390 passengers, double ended (https://www.eyemarine.com/vessel/halifax-transit-passenger-ferries/)
- Ferry colours, from the photo: dark blue hull, white upper works, yellow panels, blue HALIFAX lettering (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Halifax_Harbour_Ferry_Viola_Desmond.jpg)
- Georges Island lighthouse: octagonal concrete tower, 16 m, built 1917. White, red vertical stripe, red dome (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Island_Lighthouse)
- Georges Island: a drumlin, with Fort Charlotte (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Island_(Nova_Scotia))
- Macdonald Bridge: suspension bridge, opened April 2, 1955, 1.3 km, towers 102.9 m (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angus_L._Macdonald_Bridge)
- Bridge colour: green, old coats have gone darker (https://www.thecoast.ca/news-opinion/broken-964287/)
- Purdy's Wharf: two towers, 74 m and 88 m, built over the water on pilings, 1985 and 1989 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdy%27s_Wharf)
- Purdy's Wharf glass colour: unverified in text. The model thumbnail shows blue-grey glass.

## 7. Asset sources: licence and account

### 7.1 3D Warehouse (read this first)
- Licence: 3D Warehouse General Model License. Not Creative Commons.
- Allowed: download, adapt, publish, use inside a larger "Combined Work" that adds substantial content (https://embed-3dwarehouse-classic.sketchup.com/tos/)
- Not allowed: distributing a single model on its own, or aggregating models for redistribution (same page)
- FAQ: commercial use is fine if models "are not transferred or sold as stand-alone items". Putting models on a website for members to use is "impermissible aggregation" (https://help.sketchup.com/en/3d-warehouse/3d-warehouse-terms-use-faq)
- Attribution: not required by the licence (terms page above).
- Open question for the site: a browser scene that serves each model as its own file may count as standalone distribution. Merging into one scene file is closer to a Combined Work. Not legal advice.
- Account: Trimble ID sign-in needed to download other people's models (https://help.sketchup.com/en/3d-warehouse/managing-your-3d-warehouse-profile)
- Formats in each record: SKP, GLB, USDZ. Observed once: the GLB link in the public record answered without sign-in.
- Search used: https://3dwarehouse.sketchup.com/warehouse/v1.0/entities?q=QUERY&contentType=3dw

### 7.2 Sketchfab
- Licence is per model. All picks below show CC BY 4.0 unless marked.
- Account: free account and sign-in needed to download (https://www.itechguides.com/how-to-download-sketchfab-models-official-steps-formats-licenses-and-fixes/)
- Formats: glTF, GLB, USDZ, plus the source file (API records).
- Search used: https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/search?type=models&downloadable=true&q=QUERY
- Skipped: anything marked "Free Standard", NonCommercial or NoDerivs.

## 11. Open city data

- OpenStreetMap: Open Database License. Must credit OpenStreetMap and state the data is under that licence (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)
- Toronto 3D Massing: building shapes for the whole city, refreshed yearly, last refresh 2025-12-05. Shapefile 2025 is 81 MB, multipatch 2025 is 145 MB. AutoCAD and SketchUp tiles through a map viewer. No account (https://ckan0.cf.opendata.inter.prod-toronto.ca/api/3/action/package_show?id=3d-massing)
- Toronto licence: the dataset record says "License not specified". The city's general licence is Open Government Licence Toronto 1.0, commercial use allowed, credit line "Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence Toronto" (https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/data-research-maps/open-data/open-data-licence/)
- Montreal Batiments 3D 2016: textured buildings in CityGML, 3DM and GDB, plus DWG without textures. Covers Ville-Marie (downtown and Old Montreal), Plateau, Sud-Ouest, Outremont, Verdun, CDN-NDG. Roof accuracy 30 to 40 cm. CC BY 4.0. File sizes not shown (https://donnees.montreal.ca/dataset/batiment-3d-2016-maquette-citygml-lod2-avec-textures2)
- Vancouver Building footprints 2009: 124,181 outlines from LiDAR, with roof type, base height, top height and height above ground. Not updated (https://opendata.vancouver.ca/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/building-footprints-2009)
- Vancouver Building Footprints 2015: 154,124 outlines, flat only, no heights (https://opendata.vancouver.ca/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/building-footprints-2015)
- Vancouver LiDAR 2022: 181 tiles in LAS, 16.45 MB to 2.74 GB each (https://opendata.vancouver.ca/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/lidar-2022)
- Vancouver licence name on each record: Open Government Licence Vancouver. Licence text not opened.
- Halifax Buildings: footprints, rooflines, pedways and podiums, with use tables. Licence field says "Open Data Licence". Licence text not opened. Heights: unverified (https://www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/1f73c9ad861a4fd9998c097d3544c08e?f=json)
