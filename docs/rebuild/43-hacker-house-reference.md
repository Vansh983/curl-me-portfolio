## Verdict

- **Floqer's real house is public.** 16 photographs saved from his own and the team's LinkedIn posts (`floqer-01` to `floqer-16`). They outrank everything below.
- **Bean's house: nothing public.** beantheapp.com says only "cramped student apartments". No room photograph found.
- 19 photographs of other real hacker houses saved (`01` to `19`).

What makes a room read as a hacker house, not an office or a set:

1. Every desk is one person's and differs from the next: its own monitor, keyboard, open laptop, bottle, snack, charger. An office has the same kit in a grid.
2. Desks stand against walls and windows. Monitor backs face the wall, chair backs face the room (`floqer-01`, `floqer-03`, `floqer-04`, `09`).
3. A bed within three metres of a desk, unmade (`03`).
4. Leads show: off the back of each desk to a power strip on the floor, one extension lead across open floor (`01`, `19`).
5. Drinks in bulk: cans on desks, cases stacked on the floor, a full fridge (`floqer-07`, `floqer-08`).
6. House furniture does office work: a sofa, a plank table, a bean bag, mismatched chairs.
7. Things lie where they were dropped: hoodie on a chair back, backpack at a desk leg, shoes at the door, boxes and a suitcase still packed.
8. Handwriting: a whiteboard on wheels thick with marker and sticky notes, paper taped to a wall (`floqer-02`, `13`).
9. The company mark is home-made: foam tiles, paint, a printed plate (`floqer-06`, `14`).
10. House light: pot lights or one ceiling fitting, one floor lamp, blinds at uneven heights, monitor glow.
11. Nothing is squared, level or new. Chairs are turned, a cushion still has its tag.

## Floqer's house: what the photographs show

Facts, all from the posts listed in the README.

- A ground-floor house in downtown Toronto with a backyard: brick walls under ivy, a timber fence, a tree, festoon lights. Glass towers show above the trees (`floqer-14`, `15`).
- Outside wall: grey roughcast stucco. Door sign: orange square with the white F, white plate with raised black letters, a black band of boot-log text (`floqer-11`).
- Inside walls: cream, matte. White ceiling with a dropped bulkhead and a vent. No brick inside in any photograph.
- Floor: polished concrete. Pale grey in one room, dark brown-grey with visible stones in the other.
- Windows: black steel frames in a grid of small panes; also plain picture windows onto the garden. White and beige venetian blinds, a black mesh roller blind. They look onto trees and a block wall, not a skyline.
- Light: recessed pot lights, plus one black torchiere floor lamp in a corner. No pendants.
- The mark indoors: black pyramid acoustic foam tiles, 5 wide by 6 high, the F in orange tiles, on a white wall (`floqer-06`).
- Desks, three kinds: pale top on white wooden trestles with a shelf; pale top on a black two-leg standing frame; one dark stained plank table on a steel base.
- On the desks: one monitor per person on its own stand (one is a curved ultrawide), an open laptop beside or under it, a white rounded tower PC standing on the desk, small mechanical keyboards with coloured caps.
- Chairs: white perforated plastic stacking chairs on castors, black mesh and gaming chairs, a black shell chair on wooden legs, a navy stool.
- A double sided whiteboard on a wheeled frame: names, columns ("To-do", "Failed", "Passed", "Prod"), about 55 neon sticky notes.
- A grey tufted sofa with cushions. A dark brown bean bag. A softbox light and microphone at the plank table.
- A stainless fridge packed with energy drinks, sports drinks and cola. Cases of cans stacked on the floor.
- Words only, no photograph: the founder "bought them air mattresses" when the team moved from Halifax; a "wall quote" went up before the party; "fifa".

Where the present room differs (his call, he likes the room): parquet floor, a dark brick wall with a painted mark, pendant lamps, long shared tables, windows over rooftops to the skyline.

On the desk:

- One external monitor per person, 24 to 34 inch, on its own stand. No monitor arms in Floqer's photographs; one double pole mount in `01`.
- The monitor stands in the back third of the desk, its back to the wall or window, tilted back about 5 degrees, centred on the chair.
- The laptop is open beside the monitor on a stand, turned in 15 to 20 degrees toward the chair, or in front of the monitor and below it.
- Keyboard 10 to 15 cm in from the front edge, mouse to its right. Keyboards are short (30 to 32 cm) with coloured caps.
- A tower PC stands on the desk at one end (Floqer) or on the floor by the leg (`01`).
- Per seat, two to five of: tall insulated tumbler, can, sports drink bottle, paper coffee cup, chips bag, sticky note pad, pen, headphones lying flat, phone, tissue box, stickers.

Cables:

- Two leads drop off the back edge behind each monitor. A white laptop charger brick sits on the desk or floor.
- One power strip per desk, on the floor under the back edge or on the desk behind the monitor.
- Toronto means North American strips: a white or black bar about 30 x 5 x 4 cm, six outlets in a row, one red rocker switch, a 1.8 m lead.
- Leads hang in slack curves, five to eight per desk. One white extension lead crosses open floor to a wall socket.

Under and beside the desk: the power strip, a white plastic bin, a backpack against the leg, slides or shoes, a box, the bean bag.

What the captures of the present room show (`.cache/floqer-r0/f16.3`, `f16.9`):

- Monitors stand in two rows 30 cm apart, so beside every lit screen there is a dark back. This is what reads as "facing the wrong way". Test: put the camera at each chair's head; the screen in front of it must be lit and square to it.
- All eight seats have the same monitor on the same tall pole and the same keyboard. No laptops, bottles or leads. That reads as a lab.
- Tops look about 8 cm thick and 3 m long. Real tops are 2.5 to 4.5 cm thick and 1.4 to 1.8 m long.

## Sleeping

Photograph `03` is the one to copy: a founder's air mattress three metres from his desk.

- Size: twin 99 x 191 cm (full 137 x 191, queen 152 x 203). Raised kind 40 to 46 cm tall; low kind 22 to 25 cm.
- Shape: not a box. Top and bottom edges rounded (radius 6 to 8 cm). The side wall bulges 2 to 3 cm. Raised kinds have a seam round the middle, a slight waist.
- Top: flocked, matte, a shade lighter than the sides. A raised rim 8 to 10 cm wide round the edge. Inside the rim either rows of round dimples (about 5 across by 8 long on a twin) or lengthwise ribs 12 to 15 cm wide.
- Colours seen: grey top on charcoal sides (`03`). Navy with a grey top and beige are the other common ones.
- Pump: a black panel about 16 x 13 cm set into the side at the foot end, a round dial, a small cord door, a black lead to the wall or a power strip.
- Bedding: a white or grey duvet thrown back in a heap with deep folds, never a flat slab. One or two dented pillows that do not match. A sleeping bag is the other option.
- On the floor beside it: a phone on a white charger lead, a water bottle, slides, a backpack, an open suitcase.
- Place: head to a wall or in a corner, 2 to 4 m from a desk, out of the walkway, each at its own angle. Never a row.
- The present room's mattress (`f17.1`): a navy block, a square white slab, a box pillow, three the same. It needs the rim, the flock, the rounded sides, the pump and a rumpled duvet, and each one different.
- Mattress straight on the floor (`05`), a couch made up as a bed (`04`), bunk beds (`15`): other houses. No evidence for Floqer.

## For this room

Must have:

- Every screen lit toward its own chair; one row of monitors, not two.
- Mixed monitors (one curved ultrawide), an open laptop beside most of them.
- Leads off the back of each table to power strips on the floor; one extension lead across the floor.
- Two to five small things at each seat (see the desk list). Different at each seat.
- Thinner, shorter table tops. One black standing desk among the trestles.
- The air mattress rebuilt as above, with a rumpled duvet. Two, not three, each different.
- A hoodie on two chair backs, a backpack at two desk legs.
- Cases of cans stacked against a wall.
- The whiteboard on wheels with sticky notes.
- A black torchiere in a corner. Blinds at uneven heights.
- A grey sofa and a dark brown bean bag.

Nice to have:

- The foam-tile mark (the real one) in place of the painted sign.
- A white rounded tower PC standing on one desk.
- The door plate with its boot-log text, by the door.
- The open fridge of cans. He cut appliances from the Bean room, so ask first.
- An orange and black Floqer box, a paper takeout bag, two pizza boxes, a chips bag, a tissue box.
- A softbox light and a microphone at one table.
- Pot lights in place of the pendants. Concrete in place of parquet.

Do not add:

- People.
- Bunk beds, tents, neon, coloured light strips: other houses or no house.
- Posters, flags or slogans. The "wall quote" is real but its words are not public: do not invent them.
- Teammates' names on the whiteboard, or the street address on the door plate, without asking him. Both are in his public posts.
- Office things: dividers, matching task chairs, a reception desk, ceiling tiles, strip lights.
- European power strips or sockets.
- A router, microwave, kettle, fan or drying rack as facts of his house: none is in a photograph.
- Tidiness: rows, right angles, pairs.
