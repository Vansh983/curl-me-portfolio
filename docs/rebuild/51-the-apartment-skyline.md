# 51. The apartment's CN Tower: a photograph for a day, then the close-up in Floqer's colour (2026-10-18, 19)

He said the CN Tower and skyline in the first room were different from Floqer's and he liked Floqer's better: make it similar. Floqer's is a photograph (doc 42, 44). The apartment's was the coded city of doc 19: the tower a white stick with a pod, close, in front of the hero text.

## Round one: a photograph, rejected

- Built the way Floqer's is: "Toronto at Night" by Steve (Flickr mrlaugh, 2019, from Centre Island, CC BY-SA 2.0) cut to a strip, hung on a curved wall 2 km out, the tower 16 degrees tall in the middle pane, the coded blocks kept only below the eye, the night sky dome given the strip's dusk colours so the strip's end did not read as a poster's edge (mirrored, the Rogers Centre stood twice).
- Seen in his Chrome. His words: "no this is too fake. It has to be a close-up like it was earlier. The CN Tower should be very, very close to me. It should just be the same color as it was in the background earlier. That's all, not the white."
- So what he liked in Floqer's was the tower's colour, not the photograph. The strip, its constant, the dome's dusk and the credit were taken out again; the day strip and `buildSkyline` (now a table, `SKYLINES`, with Floqer's entry) stay.

## Round two: the close-up, in the photograph's colour

- The coded city is back as it was: every block, the tower close with its legs, pod, SkyPod and antenna, the dome at its foot.
- First read as the night photograph's red: "CN Tower is still not the same." The background he meant is Floqer's: its tower is a cool grey concrete in the day's haze (#8393ab at the SkyPod, #5e7591 above the pod, #45607e low down; the pod's decks #788ba4; the antenna #94a4bd).
- The tower's materials changed, nothing else: `cnShaft` from white (#D6D8DE) to #7E8CA2, `cnPod` from #F4F1EA to #A9B3C2. The vertex shading of `cnTower` still gives the fins their form, darker on the sides, as the photograph's shaft is.
- No rebake (the city is never baked). The opening picture remade. Tests and the build clean; `npm run check` carries three errors from the other session's pick-up work in stage-run.ts, not from this.

## What was seen

- Headless, chapter 0, with the cards: the tower grey from foot to antenna, the pod paler, close, half behind the hero text as before; the lit blocks and the dome unchanged.
- His Chrome: the tab reloaded on the page.

## Lesson

- The same as the degree (2026-10-02): improve the thing, do not swap it. "Make it similar" meant the colour.
