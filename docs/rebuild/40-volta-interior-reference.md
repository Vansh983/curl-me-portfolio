# 40. Volta interior reference

What Volta really looks like, from 25 photos in `docs/rebuild/ref/volta/` (sources and credits in its README). All 25 show the current space: Armoyan Centre, 1800 Argyle Street, Suite 801, 8th floor, opened 14 Nov 2023, about 20,000 sq ft. Collected 2 Oct 2026. Hex values are sampled from the photos and rounded by eye.

## Verdict: what makes a room read as Volta

1. A black open ceiling: deck, ducts, pipes and cable trays all painted near-black.
2. Big black ring lights with a warm glow, hung on thin cables, mixed with long black linear lights at loose angles.
3. A long window wall of glass that leans inward as it rises, with thick white mullions and a white radiator sill at knee height.
4. Outside the glass: the City Hall clock tower, office towers, the harbour and the Dartmouth shore.
5. Fat white square columns down the floor, some carrying a red fire bell and a small black speaker.
6. Warm grey carpet tile, wall to wall.
7. White furniture everywhere: white shell chairs on castors, pale flip-top tables on castors, white round high tables.
8. Large plants in black pots: fiddle leaf figs, tall cactus, dracaena.
9. Colour only in the lounge pieces: orange and yellow tub chairs, cream sofas with a green circle pattern, lime, teal and red cube ottomans.
10. VOLTA in black block letters on a pale wood plank wall behind a wood reception desk.
11. Event mode: red-orange stacking chairs in rows facing a plain wall, the slide projected straight on the wall, a white high table as the lectern.
12. The RBCx alcove beside the projection wall: black-framed glass, a navy "We power bold." panel, a square RBCx plaque.

The current 3D room has 1, 6 and the red chairs right. It lacks 2 to 5 and 7 to 9, which is why it reads as a box.

## The wall in his photo is not kraft

- No kraft, plywood or brick shows anywhere in the Argyle space.
- The projection wall is painted a light warm grey, about `#C2B9AC` in daylight (12, 10, 09).
- Under the warm rings at night it photographs tan, about `#B5926D` (19, 20).
- In his graded group photo it goes brown, about `#552C17` (23, same shot as `public/assets/stage/photos/demoday.jpg`).
- For an evening Demo Day scene, light a warm grey wall warmly. Do not texture it as paper or wood.

## Zones and layout

No floor plan found. Layout below is read from the photos, so positions are approximate.

- Entrance: glazed doorway with a white header sign reading VOLTA in black. A wall TV, a flip chart and a white phone booth pod stand just inside (08).
- Reception: wood desk in front of the wood VOLTA wall. Beside it a small nook with an arched window, two red and grey armchairs and a round table (06, 07).
- Kitchen bar: across from reception. A dark counter ledge on a white wall, white bar stools, toaster, plant, and zig-zag light sticks on the wall above (07). A fuller kitchen with warm-lit shelves sits behind a whiteboard partition at the back of the event area (09, likely).
- Desks with grey screens down the corridor from reception, likely the dedicated desks (07).
- Open coworking floor: runs along the sloped glass. Table groups in the middle, high tables and lounge clusters at the glass (01 to 05).
- Event area: the end of the open floor, in the corner where the sloped glass meets a second, vertical window wall. Tables are rolled away and red chairs set out (09, 14, 21 to 25).
- Projection wall: door with a "VOLTA 825" plaque and a black Bureau call booth at its left end; a rolling whiteboard, then the RBCx alcove, at its right (13, 19, 18, 12).
- RBCx alcove: bench seats with dark seats and tan back cushions, a small white table, black-framed glass (12, 10).
- Office corridor: right of the alcove. Lower flat ceiling with panel lights, green exit sign, charcoal doors with room names. One reads "Fredericton" (12, 10).
- Offices and meeting rooms: Volta lists 20 company offices, 4 partner offices, several meeting rooms and call booths. No photos of their insides found.

## Ceiling and lights

- Height: about 3.6 to 4 m to the deck, estimated from people and columns. A lower dark bulkhead, about 2.7 m, runs above the windows.
- Colour: deck and services `#1D1B1A`. Big round ducts are the same black, some wrapped (01, 16).
- Ring pendants: black body, warm white light on the inner face, about 1.5 m and about 2.5 to 3 m across, three cables up to one point. Light reads `#FFD9A6`. Many, scattered, at about 2.7 m (04, 14, 25).
- Linear pendants: slim black bars, 1.5 to 2.5 m long, neutral white `#FFF1DC`, hung at mixed angles; a straight run follows the window line (01, 03, 16).
- Black dome pendants on short stems between them, likely speakers or downlights (04, 21).
- One white projector on a ceiling pole facing the wall (11, 16).
- Reception: a cluster of short linear lights at random angles (07).

## Floor

- Carpet tile everywhere, including the event area. Fine striated texture, no visible pattern at distance.
- Colour: warm grey `#8F8677`. Reads `#AAA08D` in bright daylight and `#5A4F45` in warm evening light (12, 10, 02).
- No concrete, wood or rugs seen.

## Walls, columns, glass

- Walls: light warm grey `#C2B9AC`, dark grey baseboard. Kitchen wall and some partitions are whiter, about `#DAD5CC` (07, 09).
- Columns: white `#E6E3DC`, about 0.6 m square, grey base (01, 03).
- Wood wall: pale maple planks laid flat, mixed tones, `#BF9872` average, range `#AE8F73` to `#C9A57E`. Desk front matches, `#C49B6D` (06, 07).
- Doors: flat charcoal `#4E4E4E`, silver lever, keypad lock. Plaque beside the door: white top half with VOLTA, black bottom half with the number (13).
- Interior glass: black frames `#1E1E1E` (RBCx alcove, booths). The entrance doorway and the glass beside it have white frames (08).
- Sloped glazing: leans in about 20 to 25 degrees, white mullions `#D9D9D4` about every 1.5 m, glass continues overhead at the room edge (05, 09). Sill and radiator cover white, about 0.6 m high.
- Second window wall: vertical glass, darker mullions, faces a brick-red office block with ribbon windows `#C98F72` (14).
- No murals, neon, acoustic panels or brick.

## Furniture

- Tables: rectangular flip-top tables, pale grey woodgrain top `#B9B2A5`, white T legs on castors, pushed into groups of two to four (02, 14).
- Chairs: white plastic shell with arms `#EDEBE6`, dark grey seat pad `#3A3A3A`, four legs on castors (02).
- High tables: white round top about 0.9 m on a white column and disc base, bar height. White stools with wire sled legs (02, 07).
- Event chairs: red-orange plastic shell, chrome wire legs, stackable. `#D43D2E` in daylight, `#961E0C` at night (09, 13, 18).
- Sofas: boxy two and three seaters, cream `#D7D1B5` with a yellow-green and grey circle pattern (01, 03).
- Tub chairs: orange `#C8853A` and pale yellow `#D9B36A` (05, 03).
- Cream swivel shell chairs on four-star bases; round side table, white top on a walnut cone (05).
- Cube ottomans: lime `#A9A83C`, teal `#55C4E2`, red `#B13A2E` (03, 05).
- One arc floor lamp with a wide dark drum shade at the glass (04, 05).
- Lectern: a white high table with a laptop; a white AV cart beside it (10, 19).
- Call booths: Bureau booth with black frame and glass door by door 825 (13); white single pod near the entrance (08).

- VOLTA header over the entrance doorway (08). VOLTA plus number plaques at doors (13).
- RBCx: square white plaque `#F2EFEA` with "RBCx", and a navy panel `#1E202D` reading "We power bold." (12, 11).
- Framed black posters with white text on some columns and walls. Text not readable (16, 17).
- No sponsor wall and no logo on the projection wall.

## Plants, desks, walls

- Plants: fiddle leaf figs 2 m tall, columnar cactus by the glass, dracaena by columns, all in black cylinder pots `#1E1E1E`. Leaf green `#32411E`. Low planter boxes on the partition near the kitchen (01, 02, 09).
- On tables: laptops, bottles, coffee cups, backpacks on the floor. No fixed monitors on the open floor.
- Whiteboards: a wall of white panels near the kitchen (09) and one rolling board by the projection wall (18).

## Event setup (Demo Day, 15 Jan 2026, 6 to 9 pm)

- Slide projected straight onto the wall, about 2.5 m wide, top edge about 2.6 m up, right of the speaker (18, 19).
- Speaker stands at the left with the white high table; door 825 and the booth behind them (19, 20).
- Rows of about 8 to 10 red chairs, tight, facing the wall; windows to the audience's left and behind (21, 22, 24).
- Dark outside, glass reflects the room. Rings and linear lights stay on (22, 25).

## Build list

Must have
- Black open ceiling with ducts.
- Four or more ring lights and a scatter of linear lights.
- Sloped glass wall with white mullions and the clock tower and harbour beyond.
- White columns.
- Warm grey carpet, light warm grey walls.
- Red-orange chairs in rows facing a slide on the plain wall.
- Plants in black pots.

Nice to have
- White tables and shell chairs pushed to the side.
- One lounge cluster at the glass: orange tub chairs, patterned sofa, cube ottomans.
- Door 825 plaque and the black call booth left of the slide.
- RBCx alcove right of the slide.
- Wood VOLTA wall and desk, if the walk passes reception.
- White high table as lectern, name stickers.

Do not invent
- Kraft, plywood or brick walls.
- Murals, graffiti or neon (the murals are the old Maritime Centre space).
- A TV or pull-down screen for the slide.
- A coffee counter in the event area. The bar ledge is by reception.
- Sponsor logos other than RBCx.
- Views other than the clock tower, towers, harbour and the brick block.
