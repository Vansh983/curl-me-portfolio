# 71. A clear view of Halifax

The left window now frames the Macdonald Bridge and Halifax rooftops throughout the forward descent. The wing, engine and exhaust housing have been removed. The boarding passage and cabin have plain ceilings, with their illumination baked into the walls, aisle and seats.

Matched frames in headless Chrome showed the bridge passing the shaded window frame in Scroll. In Walk, the passenger took the left seat and arrived in the auditorium. The seven-second recording is in `.cache/round71/`.
