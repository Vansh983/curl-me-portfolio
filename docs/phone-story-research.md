**Verdict: use version 3.** The ten examples below make room for decisions, uncertainty and small moments. Keep the early years brief; give Bean and Floqer room to tell what actually happened.

**1. Moves that recur**

- **Talk to one reader.** [Michael Lynch](https://mtlynch.io/why-i-quit-google/) anticipates your reaction and corrects himself.
- **Explain the next move.** [Adam Wathan](https://adamwathan.me/going-full-time-on-tailwind-css/) connects changing direction to what he wanted.
- **Keep one concrete detail.** [Justin Jackson](https://justinjackson.ca/about) brings out his teenage event budget.
- **Admit something unfinished.** [Joel Hooks](https://joelhooks.com/blog/2014/12/24/bootstrapping-egghead-dot-io-to-feed-my-family-2014-in-review) says quitting still frightens him.
- **Allow a small aside.** [Josh Comeau](https://www.joshwcomeau.com/about-josh/) interrupts his credentials with ordinary, funny details.
- **Vary sentence length.** [Derek Sivers](https://sive.rs/about) follows a childhood memory with two words: “Still is.”
