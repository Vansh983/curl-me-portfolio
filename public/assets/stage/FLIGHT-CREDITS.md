# The flight into Halifax

- Halifax under the window: the flat drawing is derived from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). Coastline, islands, streets, bridge outlines, piers, parks, land use, inland water and building footprints retain their mapped coordinates, including the Goldberg Computer Science Building (way 373834324). The OpenStreetMap data is not an official survey or a guarantee of completeness.
- `scripts/stage-halifax-map.mjs` generates `halifax-map/*.webp` from the same Overpass source used by `scripts/stage-halifax.mjs` (overpass-api.de, with kumi.systems and private.coffee mirrors). The 2026-11-15 geometry snapshot, query, feature IDs, relevant tags and source timestamp are available as [source.json.gz](./halifax-map/source.json.gz), under ODbL. The map uses the original latitude/longitude origin, metre conversion and 340° heading; `src/lib/stage/halifax-map.json` records the frame, texture bounds and snapshot checksum. No coast or footprint is simplified. Road widths, palette, soft footprint shadows and water sheen are cartographic styling, not surveyed widths, heights or bathymetry.
- The earlier extruded city and detailed Studley campus are no longer shown out of the aircraft. Their retained data and builders (`halifax.json`, `dalhousie.json`, `halifax.ts`, `dalhousie.ts`) came from `scripts/stage-halifax.mjs`; inferred heights, roofs, the Hicks tower, Dalplex dome and extra trees were architectural interpretations.
- Source bases reported by Overpass: main extract 2026-11-15T13:18:35Z; the larger coastline extract from kumi.systems 2026-07-24T11:04:51Z. Both were fetched on 2026-11-15 and their separate timestamps are preserved. This is a trace of those mapped snapshots, not a claim of current surveyed geography.
- Unfinished source geometry: unnamed building way 1488441252 in Dartmouth (around 44.6576, -63.5411) has an open outline and no enclosing building relation in the extract. Its fill is omitted; no closing edge has been invented. The source way remains in the downloadable snapshot.
- Sky: [Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky), Greg Zaal (photography), Jarod Guest (sky edits), CC0. Tonemapped panorama resized to 4096 × 2048 WebP, now warm-graded only on its visible dome. The original panorama still supplies the cabin's environment light. The separate cloud deck is painted at runtime (`stage-paint.ts`) and tinted warm; the ground has distance haze. Golden hour is the requester's interpretation of Vansh's earlier words, not a claim about a real flight's time or route.
- Retained but no longer displayed in the flight: `dalhousie_campus.glb`, project-authored geometry made in Blender with `scripts/stage-flight-campus.py`: the Goldberg Computer Science Building alone, zinc-panel façade, recessed blue glazing, four-storey massing, rooftop equipment, entrance and forecourt, based on [Dalhousie's Goldberg reference](https://www.dal.ca/campus-maps/building-directory/studley-campus/goldberg-computer-science.html). An architectural interpretation, not a survey or an official model. Its actual OSM footprint is now drawn in the map instead.
- The aircraft, its wing and engine, the jet bridge and the seats are built in code (`src/lib/stage/built.ts`). The route on the seat-back screens is drawn, not a real flight plan.
- Trees, furnishings and surfaces retain their [existing credits](./CREDITS.md).

Rebuild the current drawing: `node scripts/stage-halifax-map.mjs` (cached extract or shipped snapshot, no network); add `--fetch` to refresh from Overpass. The original city/asset scripts remain for the older geometry and existing sky, but are not needed to regenerate this drawing. No authentication is required.
