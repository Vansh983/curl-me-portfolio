`dalhousie-goldberg.webp` is resized to 1600 pixels and converted to WebP; no other edits to the stored image. The runtime adds a location caption and crops it within the window/phone. These photographic derivatives retain CC BY-SA 4.0. Reproduce the stored image with `node scripts/stage-campus.mjs`.

Used as a photographic background outside the aircraft window and in its phone camera. The flight-to-campus transition is cinematic, not a claim that this is an actual runway view. The classroom is an authored interpretation, not a surveyed replica.
